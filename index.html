Class 1

Build a "Which One Are You?" quiz

The whole flow lives here: warm-up questions, sketching, testing, building, and uploading. Open one step at a time and keep moving forward.

1 Design Corner: Start With A Human

Kid version of the idea: great builders do not start by making stuff. They start by noticing what another person likes, needs, and finds confusing.

  • Technical term: empathy interview
  • You can also call it a user interview
  • Your job is to learn from a real human before choosing your idea
2 Interview Your Human

Ask your partner a few simple questions and write down what you learned.

  • What kinds of quizzes or games do you actually like?
  • What makes something fun for you?
  • What makes something boring, confusing, or annoying?
  • Write: what they like, what they do not like, and what surprised you
3 Pick a Theme

Now choose a theme that matches what you learned from your human.

  • Animals
  • Fantasy creatures
  • Space adventure
  • Books
  • Food
  • Your own funny idea
4 Paper Prototype First

Sketch before coding.

  • Write the quiz title
  • Invent 5 results
  • Make 5 questions with 3 choices each
  • Show the ending screen on paper
  • Take a photo and upload the paper prototype in your student slot
5 Human Test

Trade with a partner and watch quietly.

  • What was confusing?
  • What felt fun?
  • What would make the result better?
6 Build With AI

Ask AI to help you turn your paper quiz into one HTML file.

  • Keep the code beginner-friendly
  • Ask AI to explain things simply
  • Change the first draft to make it your own
7 Upload and Share

Save both versions in your upload slot.

  • Upload your paper prototype photo
  • Upload your final `index.html` or `.zip` quiz
  • Show your result screen to a friend or family member
