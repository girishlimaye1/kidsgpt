Class 1

Build a "Which One Are You?" quiz

The whole flow lives here: warm-up questions, sketching, testing, building, and uploading. Open one step at a time and keep moving forward.

1 Warm Up: What is AI?

Unlock your student slot and answer three short questions:

  • What is AI?
  • What can AI do well?
  • What is one good thing and one risky thing about AI?
2 Pick a Theme

Choose something you and your friends would actually want to try.

  • Animals
  • Fantasy creatures
  • Space adventure
  • Books
  • Food
  • Your own funny idea
3 Paper Prototype First

Sketch before coding.

  • Write the quiz title
  • Invent 5 results
  • Make 5 questions with 3 choices each
  • Show the ending screen on paper
  • Take a photo and upload the paper prototype in your student slot
4 Human Test

Trade with a partner and watch quietly.

  • What was confusing?
  • What felt fun?
  • What would make the result better?
5 Build With AI

Ask AI to help you turn your paper quiz into one HTML file.

  • Keep the code beginner-friendly
  • Ask AI to explain things simply
  • Change the first draft to make it your own
6 Upload and Share

Save both versions in your upload slot.

  • Upload your paper prototype photo
  • Upload your final `index.html` or `.zip` quiz
  • Show your result screen to a friend or family member

Upload studio

Student upload desk

Each student gets a named slot and a secret password. They can answer a few opening AI questions, upload a paper prototype photo, then upload one `index.html` file or one `.zip` bundle if their project uses images.

Teacher note: uploads are stored in this browser so you can preview and collect them. Move the final files into the repo when you are ready to publish.

Before publishing

Class safety checklist

No full names

No kid photos

No location details

No private contact info

No mean results

No official logos
