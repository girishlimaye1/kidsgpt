# Class 1: Build a "Which One Are You?" AI Quiz

## Page 1: Facilitator Guide

### Goal

Kids build a small personality quiz they can show friends and family. They learn that builders start with people, sketch before coding, use AI as a helper, test with real humans, and improve.

### Big Message

"AI can help you build faster, but you are the designer. Your job is to care about the person using it."

### Materials

- Laptops
- Paper and pencils
- AI chat access
- Optional: timer, projector, stickers/prizes

### Class Flow: 75-90 Minutes

1. Hook: 5 minutes
   - Show a silly example quiz: "Which Library Legend Are You?"
   - Ask: "Why do people like taking quizzes like this?"

2. Empathy: 10 minutes
   - Pair up.
   - Each kid asks their partner:
     - What theme would you actually want to take a quiz about?
     - What kind of result would make you laugh or feel proud?
     - What would make the quiz boring or annoying?

3. Paper Prototype: 15 minutes
   - Each kid sketches:
     - Quiz title
     - 5 possible results
     - 5 questions
     - 3 answer choices per question
     - Result screen

4. Human Test: 10 minutes
   - Partner takes the paper quiz.
   - Designer watches quietly.
   - Tester says what is confusing, funny, or exciting.

5. AI Build Sprint: 25-30 minutes
   - Kids use AI to generate a one-file HTML quiz.
   - Remind them: the first AI version is a draft.

6. Human Test Again: 10 minutes
   - Partner tries the laptop version.
   - Each kid changes one thing based on feedback.

7. AI Risk Bite: 5 minutes
   - Ask: "Can a quiz really know your personality?"
   - Lesson: AI and quizzes guess from patterns. They can be fun, but they should not label people in mean or serious ways.

8. Showcase: 10 minutes
   - Each kid shows their result screen.
   - Prompt: "I built this for ___ because ___."

### Rules for Results

- Every result should feel good to receive.
- No mean labels like lazy, dumb, annoying, ugly, or bad friend.
- Avoid official logos/images from copyrighted worlds. Fan-inspired names are okay.
- The app must be understandable to someone who has never seen it before.

### When They Get Stuck

- If AI gives too much code: "Make it simpler. I am 11 and new to coding."
- If the quiz breaks: "Find the bug in this HTML file and explain the fix simply."
- If the design is boring: "Give me 5 ways to make this quiz more fun without making the code hard."
- If they finish early: add a restart button, badges, colors, animations, or fun result descriptions.

## Page 2: Student Handout

# Build Your Own "Which One Are You?" Quiz

Today you are a designer, builder, and AI director.

Your mission: create a fun quiz that a friend would actually want to take.

## What You Will Learn Today

- How to interview someone with empathy
- How to turn an idea into a paper prototype
- How to test your design with a real human
- How to ask AI for beginner-friendly code
- How to change AI's first draft into your own creation
- How to use AI in a fun, kind, and careful way

## Meet Your AI Buddy

Your AI Buddy can help you brainstorm, code, debug, and explain things.

But remember:

- You are the designer.
- Your partner is the real user.
- AI is allowed to be wrong.
- Do not paste private information into AI.
- If AI makes something too complicated, ask it to make it simpler.

Good AI Buddy prompts:

```
Explain this like I am 11 and new to coding.
Make this quiz more fun, but keep the code simple.
Find the bug and show me exactly what to change.
Give me 5 better result names that are positive and funny.
```

- Animals
- Fantasy creatures

- Sports
- Video games
- Books

- Food
- Music

```
Which __________________ Are You?
```

## Step 6: Ask AI to Help You Build

Use this prompt:

```
I am 11 and new to coding. Help me build a fun personality quiz in one HTML file.

Theme: [your theme]

The quiz should have:
- 5 questions
- 3 answer choices for each question
- 5 possible personality results
- A result screen at the end
- A restart button
- Fun colors and simple styling
- Beginner-friendly HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Comments that explain the important parts

Here are my 5 results:
[paste your results]

Here are my 5 questions:
[paste your questions]

Please put everything in one file.
```

## Step 7: Make It Yours

Do not accept the first AI version unchanged. Make at least 3 personal edits:

- Change the title
- Change colors
- Add jokes
- Rename results
- Improve confusing buttons
- Add a fun badge
- Make the ending better

## Final Check

Before you show it:

```
[ ] My quiz works
[ ] My result screen appears
[ ] The restart button works
[ ] My friend tested it
[ ] I improved one thing after testing
[ ] No result is mean
```

Remember: AI is your helper. You are the designer.
