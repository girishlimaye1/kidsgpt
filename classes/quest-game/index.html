4 Paper Prototype

Use 10 question cards, 10 paper coins, and 3 paper hearts. Partner plays while the builder watches quietly.

  • Did the player understand what to do?
  • Were any questions too hard or too easy?
  • Did the player ever want to spend a power-up?
  • What should change before asking AI to code it?

Prompt lab

Quest prompt builder

Fill this like a worksheet. The page will write a game prompt, a character prompt, and a scene/theme prompt you can paste into AI.

1. Quest idea

Pick an original world or an inspired-by world with your own names.

2. Five scenes

Each scene needs a place, a background, and a challenger.

3. Question plan

Tell AI what kinds of questions to make. AI will create all 10 questions, choices, answers, and hints.

4. Power-ups and victory

Locked rules: 5 scenes, 10 questions, start with 3 lives, correct answer gives 1 coin, wrong answer loses 1 life, 0 lives restarts the quest.

Power-ups are used before answering: half-half costs 1, hint costs 1, skip costs 2, extra life costs 4.

Tip: after the game is built, test coins, hearts, restart, power-ups, and victory before publishing.
